Nigeria

Rich Flavours • Vibrant Culture • Unforgettable Places

From spicy street food to hearty traditional dishes, Nigeria's food scene is bold, flavourful and full of life.

Popular Nigerian Foods

Nigerian Jollof Rice

Long-grain rice cooked in a tomato, red pepper and onion base with spices and stock.

Pounded Yam and Egusi Soup

Smooth pounded yam served with rich egusi soup made from ground melon seeds, leafy greens, stock, meat or fish.

Suya

Thinly sliced beef or chicken coated in yaji, a spicy groundnut-and-pepper seasoning, then grilled on skewers.

Kenya

Coastlines • Wildlife • Rich Traditions

Explore Kenya's diverse cuisine and stunning landscapes, from the coast to the savannah.

Popular Kenyan Foods

Smocha

A soft chapati rolled around a boiled sausage or sometimes a boiled egg.

Omena

Small, dry fish cooked with onions, tomatoes and spices.

Chicken Biryani

Fragrant rice cooked with chicken and aromatic spices.

Mokimo

Mashed greens, potatoes, peas and maize, often served with meat.

Top 4 Places to Visit in Kenya

Mombasa(Coast)

Maasai Mara (National Reserve)

Hell's Gate National Park

Tsavo East

Tanzania

Mountains • Wildlife • Islands

From the heights of Kilimanjaro to the beaches of Zanzibar, Tanzania offers unforgettable experiences.

Popular Tanzanian Foods

The Home of Vegetables.

Ugali

Tasty vegetable leaves,best served with ugali.

Kisamvu

Cassava leaves cooked with onions, tomatoes and spices.

Mlenda

An amazing ,delicious meal of Fresh cassava leaves cooked with coconut milk and spices.

Uji wa Mtama

Finger millet porridge, rich,nutritious and tasty.

Top 4 Places to Visit in Tanzania
